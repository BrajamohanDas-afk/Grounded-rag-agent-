# Battery Storage

Lithium-ion batteries dominate grid-scale storage because of their high energy density and falling costs. Battery pack prices fell by roughly 90 percent between 2010 and 2023.

Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) chemistry is increasingly popular for stationary storage because it is safer and lasts for more charge cycles than nickel-based chemistries, although it stores less energy per kilogram.

Round-trip efficiency measures how much energy you get back compared to what you put in. Modern lithium-ion systems typically achieve round-trip efficiencies of 85 to 95 percent.

Pumped hydro storage remains the largest form of grid energy storage worldwide by installed capacity.
