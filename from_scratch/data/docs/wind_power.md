# Wind Power

Wind turbines convert the kinetic energy of moving air into electricity. The power available in wind is proportional to the cube of wind speed, so doubling wind speed increases available power eightfold.

The Betz limit states that no wind turbine can capture more than 59.3 percent of the kinetic energy in wind.

Offshore wind farms benefit from stronger and more consistent winds than onshore sites, but they cost more to build and maintain.
